# Story coherence audit — V2.2 message corpus

Read of all 54 messages in delivery order against `canon.md`, `canon-timeline.md`,
`facts.csv` and all 20 booklets. 2026-08-19.

**Verdict: the story holds.** The three identity cases are separately sourced, the
Vale incident chain escalates across three holders as designed, and the corporate
thread interlocks tightly. Four defects below; two are worth fixing before a
playtest.

---

## What works

**The stylistic arc (RH15) is correctly split.** Early R (first person, the
tide-table metaphor, sensory detail that checks out) sits with Frankie in V2M042.
Middle R (plural sourcing, three margin hands) sits with Tessa in V2M043. Late R
(uniform cadence, a printing floor described years after demolition) sits with Edi
in V2M044. Three inboxes, three sections, no one holds the arc assembled — exactly
what `canon-timeline.md` requires.

**The One Human chain is now genuinely resilient.** V2M042 (the copy desk's
physical verification) → V2M053 (the tease: fourteen years of handwritten
challenges) → V2M039 (the source: twenty-two of twenty-two, three countries, a rule
never written down). It survives the mantle rebuttal on its own terms, which is
what the V2.1 rebalance was for.

**The escalating Vale chain (VT16–18) lands in three different hands and each link
keeps an innocent reading.** Electronic (V2M046, Samira — a session from a device
Vale never owned, a paragraph that improved itself). Observational (V2M047, Morgan
— the same camera vehicle on two streets, plate resolving to a dissolved shell).
Physical (V2M048, Dee — a delivery robot off its filed route). Assembled in order
it makes autonomous action arguable; no single holder can get there alone.

**The corporate thread interlocks precisely.** V2M016 tells Celeste that DoITT has
pulled her calendar twice, once not at her request. V2M015 tells Mira her signature
is on the parent authorisation but no access grant, and that the credential was
raised "per City Hall" with no calendar entry on either side. V2M033 gives Celeste
a deadline of tonight. V2M019 has the Parallax programme officer mention the city
asked about the gift language in July. Four inboxes, one consistent set of facts,
no character holding more than a quarter of it.

**V2M013 and V2M015 are a deliberate pair.** Eli is told to ask Sen for the
authorisation chain rather than the approval, "the one with the second signature."
Mira has just been told in her own mail that eleven grants were countersigned under
delegated authority. Neither knows the other received their half.

**Kit's redemption arc paces correctly.** Tip line artifact (V2M049) → invitation
theory disproved in public (V2M008, registration marks from a tired press) → ledger
theory disproved in public (V2M023, the recurring charge tracks the egg index) →
one true, checkable correlation he must hand to other people to be believed
(V2M054). Two public failures buy the third.

## Defects

### 1. HIGH — the messages gender characters the booklets deliberately leave open

Every booklet is written pronoun-neutral so any player can take any role. The
messages break that for ten of sixteen core characters.

Honorifics: `Mr. Shaw` (V2M047), `Mr. Lowell` (V2M002, V2M017), `Ms. Velez`
(V2M014), `Ms. Park` (V2M015, V2M016), `Ms. Kwan` (V2M017), `Mr. Navarro`
(V2M017), `Mr. Calderón` (V2M021), `Mr. Pike` (V2M052), `Ms. Nowak` (V2M036).

Pronouns: V2M002 ("puts **him** somewhere other than the desk **he** describes" —
Frankie), V2M009 ("what Rafi does when **he** is scared"), V2M022 ("the engineer…
**he** is the one not talking to anybody" — Eli).

Consequence: cast a woman as Frankie and her first piece of mail calls her
Mr. Lowell. This silently removes the casting flexibility the booklets were built
to preserve.

**Also: V2M051 genders Vale** — "the question you asked **him**". Vale is
ungendered in the other 53 messages and throughout canon. This is the one to fix
first; Vale's indeterminacy is load-bearing for Ballot 2.

Fix: drop honorifics to full names, and rewrite the four pronoun instances.

### 2. HIGH — an identifier collision shortcuts Ending 4

The building's claims reference for the robot/bicycle contact is `HS-4471-C`
(V2M048, Dee's inbox). Vale's revoked Parallax credential is `ext-4471` (V2M029
Eli, V2M031 Mira).

Players who compare artifacts will read the shared number as a hard link between
the machine that struck Vale's bicycle and Vale's own credential — i.e. they get
"an autonomous system acted against Vale" handed to them. `canon.md` guardrail 7
requires Ending 4 to be assembled from three independent keys and never delivered.

Fix: change one of the two numbers. Nothing else depends on either value.

### 3. MEDIUM — every character is below the roster's own message floor

`roster.md` specifies four to six personal messages per character across the game.
Actual counts: Kit 4; Frankie, Tessa, Edi, Rafi, Dee, Samira, Morgan, Arden,
Maren, Graham 3; Jules, Mira, Eli, Celeste, Inez 2.

Distribution is also lumpy. Jules, Mira, Eli and Celeste — the four who carry the
Parallax, succession and autonomy material — receive **nothing until Act 2**. Their
booklets are live but no mail puts them in motion while Frankie, Tessa, Rafi and
Kit are being fed from the opening.

Consequence: Act 1 belongs to the origin-and-mantle question, and the synthetic
case arrives late. Given V2.1 existed to revive One Human against a Mantle
attractor, this risks over-correcting in the other direction.

Fix: one Act 1 personal message each for Jules, Mira, Eli and Celeste. That also
brings four characters up to the floor.

### 4. LOW — "Partner" reads as Morgan's partner

V2M035 lists Morgan's three needed accounts as "Building. / Neighbourhood. /
Partner." Samira is *Vale's* former partner. As PI shorthand it is defensible, but
a player reading fast will attach it to Morgan.

## Carried forward from the rewrite pass

- **V2M034 has no in-world sender who could plausibly know.** It requires someone
  aware of both Morgan's route board and that Samira personally chose that corner.
  That list is very short and none of them would text anonymously.
- **Ballot 3's "voluntarily vanished" rests almost entirely on Samira.** V2M027 and
  V2M034 both land in her inbox. If she plays it close, the option has no
  independent voice. Inez holds the neighbourhood half but has no message
  activating it as an *escape* reading rather than an access-gap reading.
- **V2M024's confession triangle may be too self-defeating to fire.** It tells each
  of three people that two others exist, then undercuts the exercise. Samira can
  corroborate all three statements but receives no prompt tying her to the beat.
