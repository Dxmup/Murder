# Story coherence audit — V2.2 message corpus

Read of all 54 messages in delivery order against `canon.md`, `canon-timeline.md`,
`facts.csv` and all 20 booklets. 2026-08-19.

**Verdict: the story holds.** The three identity cases are separately sourced, the
Vale incident chain escalates across three holders as designed, and the corporate
thread interlocks tightly. Four defects below; two are worth fixing before a
playtest.

---

## What works

**The stylistic arc (RH15) is correctly split.** Early R (first person, the
tide-table metaphor, sensory detail that checks out) sits with Frankie in V2M042.
Middle R (plural sourcing, three margin hands) sits with Tessa in V2M043. Late R
(uniform cadence, a printing floor described years after demolition) sits with Robin
in V2M044. Three inboxes, three sections, no one holds the arc assembled — exactly
what `canon-timeline.md` requires.

**The One Human chain is now genuinely resilient.** V2M042 (the copy desk's
physical verification) → V2M053 (the tease: fourteen years of handwritten
challenges) → V2M039 (the source: twenty-two of twenty-two, three countries, a rule
never written down). It survives the mantle rebuttal on its own terms, which is
what the V2.1 rebalance was for.

**The escalating Vale chain (VT16–18) lands in three different hands and each link
keeps an innocent reading.** Electronic (V2M046, Sam — a session from a device
Vale never owned, a paragraph that improved itself). Observational (V2M047, Morgan
— the same camera vehicle on two streets, plate resolving to a dissolved shell).
Physical (V2M048, Dee — a delivery robot off its filed route). Assembled in order
it makes autonomous action arguable; no single holder can get there alone.

**The corporate thread interlocks precisely.** V2M016 tells Celeste that DoITT has
pulled her calendar twice, once not at her request. V2M015 tells Nina her signature
is on the parent authorisation but no access grant, and that the credential was
raised "per City Hall" with no calendar entry on either side. V2M033 gives Celeste
a deadline of tonight. V2M019 has the Parallax programme officer mention the city
asked about the gift language in July. Four inboxes, one consistent set of facts,
no character holding more than a quarter of it.

**V2M013 and V2M015 are a deliberate pair.** Eli is told to ask Sen for the
authorisation chain rather than the approval, "the one with the second signature."
Nina has just been told in her own mail that eleven grants were countersigned under
delegated authority. Neither knows the other received their half.

**Kit's redemption arc paces correctly.** Tip line artifact (V2M049) → invitation
theory disproved in public (V2M008, registration marks from a tired press) → ledger
theory disproved in public (V2M023, the recurring charge tracks the egg index) →
one true, checkable correlation he must hand to other people to be believed
(V2M054). Two public failures buy the third.

## Defects

### 1. RESOLVED (2026-08-21) — the messages gendered characters the booklets leave open

Every booklet is written pronoun-neutral, and `roster.md` states names "can be
recast across gender", so any player can take any role. The messages broke that for
ten of sixteen core characters through honorifics (`Mr.`/`Ms.` before a cast
surname in V2M002, V2M014, V2M015, V2M016, V2M017, V2M021, V2M036, V2M047, V2M052)
and four pronoun instances (V2M002 Frankie, V2M009 Tony, V2M022 Eli, and V2M051,
which gendered **Vale** — the one that mattered most, since Ballot 2 depends on
Vale staying indeterminate).

Fixed in the V2.3 rename pass: honorifics dropped to bare surnames, the three cast
pronouns rewritten to they/them or removed, and V2M051's "the question you asked
him" changed to "the question you asked V". Remaining gendered pronouns in the
corpus all belong to NPCs (the dismissed worker, the anonymous caller, Dennis, the
property lawyer, Ruben) and are intentional.

### 2. RESOLVED (2026-08-21) — identifier collisions shortcut Ending 4

The building's claims reference for the robot/bicycle contact was `HS-4471-C`
(V2M048, Dee) while Vale's revoked Parallax credential is `ext-4471` (V2M029 Eli,
V2M031 Nina). Players comparing artifacts would have read the shared number as a
hard link between the machine that struck Vale's bicycle and Vale's own
credential — handing them "an autonomous system acted against Vale", which
`canon.md` guardrail 7 requires the room to assemble.

A second, quieter collision sat beside it: Lodestar's `vendor agreement 7712` in
the same notice is one digit from `MID 44-7719-C`, the merchant intermediary in
Robin's settlement (V2M014), which would falsely tie the performance payment to
the delivery-robot vendor.

Fixed: the claims reference is now `HS-3062-C` and the vendor agreement `5530`.
`ext-4471` is unchanged and remains consistent across its three appearances,
which is exactly what the account association establishes and all it establishes.

### 2b. RESOLVED (2026-08-21) — the Act 3 broadcast delivered the Vale sequence

V2M025 opened Act 3 by giving all twenty players the Vale-incident chronology in
order — on the block, the service door during the camera gap, the automated
intervention, then nothing — carrying five facts including `V2F033`, which
existed nowhere else. It refused the conclusions but still removed the assembly
work for a whole Ending 4 key, and it is the likely mechanism behind run finding 4
(dead ballot lines; "voluntarily vanished" winning through an uncontested anchor):
a room handed one narrative simultaneously has nothing left to trade.

Rewritten to create the work instead of doing it. The sender now says four people
each hold one true thing, that at least two will keep it pocketed until asked
directly, and explicitly refuses to supply the sequence — "if I hand you a
sequence you will believe the sequence, and you will believe it because I gave it
to you in order." `V2F033` moved to Dee's V2M028, where Ruth already argues the
two events have different causes "so far as anybody can currently demonstrate".

Verified afterwards: no message carries facts from more than one Ending 4 key.
The three keys sit with six, six, and five holders respectively.

### 3. RESOLVED (2026-08-21) — every character was below the message floor

`roster.md` specifies four to six personal messages per character. Counts were
2–4, and Jules, Nina, Eli and Celeste — the four carrying the Parallax,
succession and autonomy material — received nothing at all until Act 2, so the
corporate half of the story had no advocate while the origin-and-mantle half was
being fed from the opening.

Seventeen messages added (V2M055–V2M071), taking every character to four and
closing the Act 1 dead zone: all four of those characters now have pre-loaded and
Act 1 mail. Per-act personal counts are now 16 / 17 / 17 / 14.

The additions are world-building and subgoal pressure, not mystery evidence.
None carries a `fact_ref`, and none bears on R's identity, Vale's fate, or the
article. They do carry small, concrete levers for the secondary and tertiary
goals — the vesting Nina can restore tonight without a board resolution, the
three other agencies whose procurement paperwork makes Celeste's diversion true,
the anthology that has no rights holder to sign, the contract clause Robin can
refuse loudly, the 2011 work order that never existed and gives Dee standing
against the twelfth floor, the four written asks Rosa can trade for.

They also carry the lore that grounds the setting: Local 3 chartered in 1947 for
the compositors on those same four floors and arguing in 1951 about a machine
that did the work of six men; the service crossing being the width of a paper
roll because it was the paper feed; the three hundred and ten who worked that
floor, eleven of whom lived on the block.

Cost: per-player reading rises from ~1,870 to ~2,280 words, about 12.7 minutes
across a three-hour party, roughly 7% of the evening.

### 4. LOW — "Partner" reads as Morgan's partner

V2M035 lists Morgan's three needed accounts as "Building. / Neighbourhood. /
Partner." Sam is *Vale's* former partner. As PI shorthand it is defensible, but
a player reading fast will attach it to Morgan.

## Carried forward from the rewrite pass

- **V2M034 has no in-world sender who could plausibly know.** It requires someone
  aware of both Morgan's route board and that Sam personally chose that corner.
  That list is very short and none of them would text anonymously.
- **Ballot 3's "voluntarily vanished" rests almost entirely on Sam.** V2M027 and
  V2M034 both land in her inbox. If she plays it close, the option has no
  independent voice. Rosa holds the neighbourhood half but has no message
  activating it as an *escape* reading rather than an access-gap reading.
- **V2M024's confession triangle may be too self-defeating to fire.** It tells each
  of three people that two others exist, then undercuts the exercise. Sam can
  corroborate all three statements but receives no prompt tying her to the beat.
