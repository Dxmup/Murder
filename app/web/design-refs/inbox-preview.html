Sign in

Frankie signs in with frankie.lowell@oldmetrodesk.net and the password batteredpressbadge — the press badge Frankie still carries, the object that says I was there before Frankie says a word.

No mailbox exists behind that address. It is a username, nothing more. The domain avoids naming the defunct newspaper, which canon deliberately leaves unnamed.

The inbox

Three groups, and the split is the whole point. Your briefing holds the character booklet. Tonight is live mail, stamped with the clock it actually arrived on. Kept mail is older correspondence.

Archival mail carries an amber rail and an ARCHIVE chip holding its narrative date — Six days before the anniversary, Fifteen years ago, refiled last month. A fifteen-year-old clipping can never read as something that just landed.

A message from tonight

From, To, Arrived. The From line authenticates the displayed account and nothing else, and the caveat under it says so in plain words.

Provenance can only ever be weakened here, never strengthened. An archived copy says the original is not here to compare against; a recovered message says its author and date are unverified.
