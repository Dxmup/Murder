## Overall verdict

Mixed structural integrity with severe ballot concentration on the human_mantle hypothesis (10/16 votes), while preserving character agency, voluntary object transfers, and article ambiguity.

## What worked

- Voluntary object showing and transfer (O01, O03, O04, O11) drove organic face-to-face bargaining without forced transfers or illegal authentication exploits.
- Article existence and fate belief means remained genuinely uncertain across sections (hovering around 40-48%), respecting design ambiguity.
- Characters successfully negotiated secondary and supporting goals, such as Arden (C01) and Inez (C16) settling Veridian neighborhood expenses and Maren (C07) protecting worker boundaries.

## What broke

- Severe ballot concentration on human_mantle (10 votes) while synthetic_origin received only 1 vote, violating the equal structural weight design intent.
- Progressive trajectory was overly reliant on prompt artifact loops and isolated agent performance, such as Kit Rakes (C15) executing mandatory public retractions.

## Character findings

- C15 (Kit Rakes) successfully executed progressive difficulty and self-correction by publicly retracting ungrounded theories (invitation key and bodega cipher) before establishing archive correlations.
- C13 (Samira) and C14 (Morgan Shaw) effectively navigated the mystery while protecting privacy boundaries and stripping manufactured client leads.
- C01 (Arden) and C16 (Inez) independently established concrete material trade-offs and oversight committees.

## Mechanic findings

- The deterministic ballot distribution revealed an asymmetric evidentiary pull toward collaborative production over synthetic origin.
- Object custody and show mechanics operated cleanly without automated ending triggers, preserving mechanical integrity.

## Priority changes

- Recalibrate agent prompt priors and initial evidence distributions to balance synthetic_origin and human_mantle hypotheses equally.
- Refine agent archetypes to reduce repetitive scripted public retractions and encourage more decentralized progressive discovery.
