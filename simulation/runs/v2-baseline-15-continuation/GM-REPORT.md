# Simulation Game Master Report

## Overall verdict

The simulation achieved robust mechanical integrity, active character agency, and effective article ambiguity, but suffered from premature convergence on the human_mantle identity thesis (11/16 ballots), while the progressive and synthetic origin hypotheses struggled for parity under current agent priors.

## Ballot and ending results

The article remained unresolved with strong consensus around Samira Vale's voluntary vanishing (12/16) and a decisive skew toward human_mantle identity selection (11/16), alongside minority selections for one_human (3), synthetic_origin (1), and progressive (1).

## What worked

- Article existence and fate successfully maintained ambiguity and uncertainty across sections, hovering in the 45-48% belief mean range.
- Bilateral show actions and voluntary object transfers (e.g., O03, O11, O12a, O13) functioned seamlessly without violating forced transfer rules.
- Progressive identity difficulty operated as intended, requiring rigorous historical ordering and resulting in a disciplined, hard-won single selection (C15).

## What broke

- Premature convergence toward the human_mantle narrative attractor crowded out synthetic_origin and progressive options by Section 3.
- Prompt artifact repetition across character dialogue templates led to formulaic phrasing ("let's compare notes").
- Evidence dimension gaps persisted, with multiple characters omitting full evidence chains for collaborative production and identity authority.

## Character findings

- C15 (Kit Rakes) successfully championed the progressive arc, self-correcting false typographic theories while maintaining structural focus despite low aggregate support.
- C01, C03, and C07 actively established strict contributor protection boundaries and enforceable neighborhood commitments.
- C02 and C04 effectively utilized physical and digital custody objects (O12a, O03) in recurring bilateral showings across sections.

## Mechanic findings

- The deterministic ballot distribution effectively measured agent convergence, revealing an asymmetry favoring middle-ground human-machine collaboration over pure origin hypotheses.
- Voluntary object showing and transfer rules functioned without mechanical friction or authority violations.
- Evidence dimension tracking highlighted specific reasoning bottlenecks where agents cited subsets of chains rather than full multidimensional proof.

## Priority changes

- Rebalance initial prompt initialization weights and evidence distribution to ensure synthetic_origin and progressive hypotheses retain competitive parity through Section 3.
- Introduce stricter divergence incentives and prompt variations to eliminate repetitive conversational dialogue templates among agents.
- Scaffold evidence dimension requirements more robustly in early sections to prevent artificial difficulty cliffs for minority identity paths.
