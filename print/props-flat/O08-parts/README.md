# O08 — Morgan's route board, as components

Print the map large and attach the rest by hand. A board where the receipts are
real paper taped on at real angles beats a printed picture of a board, and the
scale only works at size.

**Print the map at 18×24in.** That is what makes the taped items read correctly:
a till receipt is about 3in wide in life, so on an 18–24in board it lands at the
right fraction. 11×17 is the smallest that still works; below that the
hand-lettered street names stop being legible across a table. Foam board or
heavy stock — it gets handled all night.

Sticky notes: `SPACING IS TOO EVEN.` · `WHO GAVE ME THIS.` · `NOT IN ORDER.`

Use real matte tape and let it be crooked. Morgan did this at speed.

## What the board has to say, and why

Everything on it is pinned to something in the fiction. If you reprint, keep
these or the scenes stop working.

- **Times 21:14 / 21:22 / 21:30 / 21:38 / 21:46**, and the note *evenly spaced,
  4 km*. Ines Grau's message (V2M026) says the timestamps are "evenly spaced to
  the second across four kilometres of Manhattan pavement. Nobody walks like
  that." The evenness is the tell. Do not vary them to look natural.
- **The Rutledge Cut**, annotated *connector removed 2 yrs ago*. Same message:
  the path crosses a pedestrian connector decommissioned two years ago and
  pulled out of the vector data. It has to be a thing the route *crosses*, not a
  label floating nearby.
- **Segment 3 ends at Vasquez Shoe Repair and Keys.** V2M034 tells Sam: "Ask the
  investigator to show you the third segment. Not the whole board. The third
  one. You will recognise the corner. You chose it." Segment 3 must be
  unambiguous, and its corner must match the snapshot in Sam's envelope
  (`O09b`), which shows the same shopfront.
- **Segment 4 ends at 219 Harrow.** VT11 puts Vale in the neighbourhood near the
  former printing floor two days before the party.
- **Every business is invented.** The first versions used real New York
  businesses — Katz's, Rudy's Bar, Barcade — which belong to no one in this
  story, and one receipt read *219 E. Houston St.*, colliding with 219 Harrow.
  Numbers on props get compared.
